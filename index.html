Support and privacy for BitBrain.

BitBrain is a fictional idle tycoon game for iPhone. These pages explain how to get help, how purchases work, and how the game handles privacy.

Crypto Fictional

Clear by design.

BitBrain uses the visual language of crypto markets as a game theme only. There are no wallets, cash-out flows, real trading, investment services, or real cryptocurrency rewards.

$

Fictional economy

Coins, credits, rigs, boosts, charts, and rewards are gameplay items with no real-world value.

i

Apple purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed through Apple's StoreKit. BitBrain never receives payment card details.

*

Private by default

No account is required. Progress and settings are stored on device, and rewarded ads are optional boosts.

Need help?

For support, App Store review questions, or privacy requests, contact the developer directly.
