Privacy policy for BitBrain.

BitBrain is a fictional idle game. It does not require an account, does not connect to wallets, does not provide real exchange trading, does not sell user data, and does not use ad tracking.

At a glance

No account required No ad tracking No wallet access No payment card storage Local progress

Overview

BitBrain uses crypto market language and visuals as a game theme only. Gameplay balances, rigs, boosts, coins, rewards, markets, and charts are fictional game systems. They have no cash value, crypto value, wallet value, exchange value, investment value, or redeemable value.

BitBrain does not provide cryptocurrency, wallets, exchanges, trading, investment advice, securities, NFTs, gambling, cash-out, or real-money rewards.

Information stored on your device

BitBrain stores gameplay progress, tutorial state, settings, local purchase entitlement state, and related game preferences on your device. This information is used to run the game, restore your progress, and remember your preferences.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. BitBrain does not receive or store your payment card number, payment card security code, or full billing credentials.

Purchase entitlement information may be stored locally on your device so the app can unlock the gameplay items or features you purchased.

Market data

BitBrain may fetch public cryptocurrency price data from CoinGecko for in-game display. These network requests may expose standard technical information, such as IP address and User-Agent, to CoinGecko. Market data is used as gameplay flavor only and should not be treated as financial information.

Tracking and advertising

BitBrain does not currently use third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, advertising identifiers, or data broker sharing.

Children

BitBrain is not directed to children. Do not use BitBrain if you are not old enough to use apps with optional in-app purchases in your country or region.
